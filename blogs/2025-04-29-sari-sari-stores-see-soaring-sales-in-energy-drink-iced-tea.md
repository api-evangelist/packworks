As the Philippines experienced its hottest year on record in 2024, Filipinos turned to sari-sari stores for relief. According to its data, well-known energy drink brand Sting recorded a whopping 83% sales increase. Meanwhile, popular powdered iced tea brand Nestea saw a significant 76% spike in sales.
